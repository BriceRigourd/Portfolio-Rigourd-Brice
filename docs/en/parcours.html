My story

17,000 km from home, learning by delivering

I didn't have all the keys at the start. Each step made me learn a new tool while delivering a real project.

  1. I · RootsNouméa, until 2021

    Leaving for energy

    I grew up in New Caledonia, on an island where energy independence and climate are anything but abstract. After a two-year engineering assistant diploma (BTS) and an ATS preparatory class in Nouméa, I travelled 17,000 km to join ESTIA, in the French Basque Country.

  2. II · The turning pointBiarritz, 2023

    Alone with a blank page

    During an internship, I was asked to build an entire application without being a VBA expert. I learned by building it, solved the puzzle of linear ramps on an on/off controller, and overcame my difficulty with long-form writing: more than 100 pages of deliverables, including a 33-page user manual.

    See PlasmaCraft

  3. III · Two frontsMontpellier & San Sebastián, 2024

    Design office and Master's in parallel

    A final-year internship at Energiter and a Master's in Smart Grids at UPV/EHU, side by side. I became the yield referent, created a bat curtailment procedure in windPRO to validate the in-house Python scripts, and coded the grid connection tool at the heart of my thesis, defended in English.

    See the grid connection tool

  4. IV · The fieldToulouse, 2025

    287 plants and the tools that were missing

    At Amarenco, I oversaw 287 plants in operation and saw that the data could not keep up. I wrote the missing tools: the PVsyst ↔ SynaptiQ quality control, then the orchestrator. Tools born from my own needs, which the department then took over.

    Quality controlOrchestrator
