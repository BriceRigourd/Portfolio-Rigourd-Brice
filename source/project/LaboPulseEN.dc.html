Pulse, a dating app

The idea

A complete Android dating app, built around two ideas of my own: Super Likes that actually say something, and a transparent profile that shows everyone's real activity.

How I built it

I wrote the specification and steered a coding agent (Emergent) for the prototype, testing each step on my Android phone. Then I took the project over on my PC with Claude and Claude Code: migration to Supabase and S3 storage, Google and email sign-in, moderation, four languages and nine numbered versions.

Six key features

A

Discovery and matching

Swipe to like or pass, a match on a mutual like, undo, “smart” discovery by shared interests, and detailed filters down to the city.

B

“Send a Vibe” Super Like

10 trending icons, each with its own line, and an optional 150-character Crush Note. On the receiving side, a tab separates the likes received from those sent.

C

Transparent profile

Likes received and given, last seen, average session length and a profile type: very selective, popular, balanced or explorer.

D

Messaging and moderation

Real-time messages, blocking and reporting from the chat, and an internal moderation queue with its case files.

E

Daily Burn and shop

15 likes and 1 Super Like free every day, +5 likes per ad watched (3 per day), visibility boost, ghost mode and subscriptions.

F

AI and trust

Bio suggestions and a compatibility score from an LLM. Google or email sign-in, tokens in secure storage, account deletion and a privacy policy.
